Einea · Made by Hope C.

Einea: exploring climate.

Hope C. tests mountain ranges and climate on a world with retrograde spin.

Read Hope's feedback

I'm messing around with CartoVox. this is Einea, and I've roughly applied the most important mountain ranges (and inverted it actually, because this world has a retrograde spin). what's surprising me is that for some reason I always had it in my head that the western part of the central continent should be more forested, and that the peninsula at the continent's far east should be much more arid. I guess I overexaggerated the strength of the rain shadow. maybe I'll mess with the settings, make the entire world a bit colder/drier and see if I like it

Map and words by Hope C. · World: Einea.
