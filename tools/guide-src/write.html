Chronicle: the world's history

Import the generated timeline, then add eras, settlements, wars, borders, migrations, routes and disasters as points, lines or areas on the map, and play them over the world. Later eras keep the lasting consequences. Give a character a birth year, and their scenes and events show their age. Lifelines draws one bar per character. Events kept from the Cataclysm Lab and the Contagion map arrive here too {new}.

Work across five lanes. Connect causes to their effects, compare a dated chapter with the realms, wars and lives around it, and read warnings about impossible travel or founding dates. Convert a date between cultures to see its festivals, seasons and moon phases.

Compendium: lore you can link

The Compendium holds a page for every named thing (realms, towns, rivers, peoples, species, faiths) with measured facts beside your own words. The generator leaves the prose blank for you rather than inventing it. Describe everything lists the fields still open, and Rename everything renames across the whole world on one sheet. Your lore adds your own pages and can start from a template, such as a writer's bible or a game master's world book.

A realm's page, with its arms, the roots of its name and where it lies.

Every people speaks a tongue with its own sounds, roots and script, and names come from them. The Lexicon steers a tongue's words, and Name forge finds names by meaning. In 1.1 the Tongue studio edits signs and grammar, exports the script as a font and traces sound changes from parent to daughter tongues. A small speaker beside a name says it aloud, using a voice made on your computer {new}.

See urban and rural population, ages and classes, harvests, military upkeep and trade, plus a dialect map of how words change across the realm. Realm and town pages describe cuisine and material culture from local produce and trade.

Chart families, marriages and reigns, choose a succession law and see who is heir and why, then print a family poster. Design a magic system, a pantheon or a faction's organisation. Each becomes a searchable page with linked diagrams that can go into the World Bible.

Create species, describe their habitats and relationships, and see where they can live. In 1.1, explore a biome's food web and estimate what follows if a species arrives or disappears {new}.

Download a World Bible as PDF or EPUB with atlas maps, place pages, history, peoples and tongues. The player edition keeps to what the campaign has revealed.

Write › Manuscript

Manuscript: write the book

A word processor with the world beside it. Choose New book, then Add chapter, and write. It saves as you go. The toolbar groups Chapter tools, formatting and Book tools.

Type [[ and a name to link a place, person or page. ⌘-click a link to open its page beside the chapter. Pin a scene to a place and a day in the world's year, and the panel shows that day's date, season, moon, weather, realm and travel time from the last scene.

Run checks flags facts out of time, names spelled two ways, impossible travel and festival days. It finds supported problems, but it can't promise the whole book agrees with the world. Insights measures sentence shape, dialogue, adverbs and repetition. In 1.1, after you rename a place, review every chapter's mentions in context and undo them together {new}.

Set a word target and a finish date, and give chapters a status and colour labels. The Outline is a planning board with story-structure templates, a story-time timeline and a corkboard. The Draft tab keeps an ideas inbox and a research shelf.

Import Word, PDF or Markdown drafts. Export Word with real footnotes, EPUB, Kindle, HTML, Markdown or Scrivener. Style sets drop caps, front and back matter, trim and running heads. Read previews printed pages. In 1.1 {new}: send a DOCX to a beta reader and review the changes that come back, refresh embedded maps after a rebuild, and lay out a full-wrap print cover with spine, barcode space and bleed.
