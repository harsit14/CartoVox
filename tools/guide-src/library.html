Library

Your worlds in the Library

The Library opens on Continue your world: the world you were last in, with Resume and shortcuts to its city builder, tabletop, map gallery and drafts. Below that are Start something new and your World library. Each world has one picture: its natural colours with relief shading, the same for globes, discs, regions and flat worlds.

Choose Generate a world, Import a map, Draw a world, A region as its own world, A disc world, A flat world or Begin with Deep Time. Each one opens the matching start in Create.

More has Rename, Duplicate, Archive and Build another like this, which reuses the world's recipe with a new seed. Deleted worlds go to Trash for 30 days, and cancelled builds for 7. Search the shelf with /, and use the arrow keys to move between cards.

A .cartovox backup holds the whole project. To move the whole Library on one computer, use {{Settings › Data & privacy › Change save folder}}. It copies your worlds and keeps the old folder as a safety copy.

Name a series and add its worlds. A world can belong to more than one series. Draw star sectors, planar gates or sea routes between worlds with travel times, and export the diagram as SVG or PDF. Anchor the worlds' calendars to each other, and type [[World name::Place]] in a chapter to link a place in another world.

 The Series hub. With two or more worlds, the diagram charts the routes between them.

Discover {new}

Build a world from Discover

Discover is a hand-picked catalogue. It has 47 globe worlds that show what CartoVox does best, from a supercontinent to a planet locked to its star. It also has region, disc and flat worlds, cities, ships, dungeons, scenes, tabletops and style packs. Everything is built on your computer from its recipe, and it all works offline.

Discover, with today's picks, themed collections and a tab for each shelf.

Filter by kind, climate (hot, cold, dry, wet or extreme) or rarity, or search every shelf by name, pitch or tag. Open a world to compare its four pictures and the build estimate, then choose Build this world. The same world appears in your Library, yours to change.

A city opens in the city builder, and a ship or dungeon in Scene Studio. A style pack shows its look on a real map first, then you can apply it to a saved world. Undo puts the old look back.

Share to Discover saves a .cvxshare file. Check its four pictures, title, pitch, credit and consent first. Send the file to a friend, or offer it for the catalogue on Discord. Opening a shared file shows its credit and a preview before anything is built.

Make

Follow a guide in Make

Make is for when you know what you want to end up with. In 1.1 it holds eight short guides {new}, to finished maps, handouts, sites, styles and collections. In 1.0.1 it has three: a playable region, maps for a chapter, and finished map artwork. Each step opens the right tool and points at its controls. You choose when to build or export.
