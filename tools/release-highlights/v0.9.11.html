Delta V10, dated 18 October 2026, comes with installers for Apple Silicon Macs, Windows x64 and Linux x64 (still glibc 2.34 or newer). Your access code carries over and the licence is unchanged (still version 2.3). Worlds made with Delta V9 open as they are, and the Library offers Update to this version for them: rivers now cut their valleys, ocean currents run as gyres, and worlds built with a plate map raise mountains along it, so updating can move terrain, rain, rivers, towns and names. The full account is in the release notes.

  • Windows: worlds that stopped accepting changes work again. On a world an earlier session had open, restoring a saved version, Apply & re-simulate and Render Studio no longer fail at once with "could not complete that request"; every previous version kept by Update to this version was affected. Saves, exports, restores, renames and rebuilds now wait a moment when a virus scanner or sync tool has a file open instead of failing with "Access is denied". Saved versions recorded before an update restore again, and a version that cannot be restored is refused straight away with the reason. Library.
  • Several GeoJSON files make one world: choose or drop several GeoJSON files or shapefiles at once. Each file's own coordinate system is read, their land is joined into one coastline, and their rivers, roads, towns and borders are kept. The import report lists every file and what was read from it; a file that cannot be placed with the others is named and refused rather than dropped. Import a map.
  • Paint your plates: the tectonic plate map gains a Painted lines format. Paint rough lines in one bright colour over any picture of your map; CartoVox finds the colour, reads thick strokes down their middles, bridges small gaps and ignores loose ends. Over a GeoJSON or shapefile import the picture fits your land by itself. Mountains now rise where your plates collide across your own continents, and plate edges drawn across a continent come out as collisions about half the time, as on Earth. The picture must be in longitude and latitude, and plate motion is still simulated, so some lines still become rifts or sliding faults. Import a map.
  • Rivers cut their valleys: river erosion now cuts about 12 m a round in the uplands and up to a couple of hundred metres on steep large rivers, the same at every map size, without leaving hollows behind. Rebuilt worlds have slightly lower ground along their rivers and a few fewer small lakes, and Rounds of erosion and River incision in the Erosion Lab now do what they say.
  • Local rebuilds wear your edit: when Apply & re-simulate rebuilds only the area around a sculpted change, that ground is worn by the world's erosion as a full rebuild would wear it, so a raised ridge comes back with rivers cut into it. A new river line rebuilds just its area, a sculpt that reaches under the sea along a coast stays local as its review promises, and the finished message says how much of the world was rebuilt. Sculpt · River lines.
  • Ocean gyres with warm currents: warm water streams poleward along the eastern coasts of the continents, as the Gulf Stream and the Kuroshio do, and cold water drifts back across the open ocean. Subtropical east coasts get humid, rainy summers, and the tropical rain belt and equatorial forests regain some of the rain they had lost.
  • Five languages almost everywhere: German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Polish now reach the welcome tour's wet-or-dry readings, Settlement Studio, the Library's update notes, the region, story-pin and realm prompts, the cell inspector, the map import workshop, Render Studio's save line and dialogs throughout the app. The campaign table's default names show in your language; what is saved stays as it was. Settings.
  • Deep Time's volcanoes no longer swamp its carbon: degassing varies about as much as Earth's, so the weathering of mountains and warm, wet land steers carbon dioxide in every history. Saved histories are unchanged until run again. Deep Time.
  • Measured again: on the reference M5 Pro Mac a Sketch world took 33 seconds, Quick 54 seconds and Full 170 seconds, inside the about 35 seconds, 55 seconds and 3 minutes Create quotes. A Low memory build with its map open took 3.24 GB, inside its 4 GB promise. These are source runs from 17 October 2026, not timings of the packaged installers. Build.
  • Also fixed: book and chapter names Windows reserves for devices (CON, NUL, AUX and the like) are refused, and places with such names get vault pages Windows can open, such as "Aux (realm)"; changing only a title's capitals works; the heightmap's Under the ice choice appears only on worlds with ice sheets; braided tributaries no longer draw gravel bars on the river they join; and an unexpected problem's message now says what went wrong.
