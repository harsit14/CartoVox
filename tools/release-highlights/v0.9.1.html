0.9.1 adds little that is new to look at. It makes the paths 0.9 put on the sheet agree with each other: a described region costs in review what it costs when you apply it, the example world reports its progress where every other build does, and the bound atlas says what it will contain before it spends minutes making it. The full account, including the release checks behind it, is in the release notes.

  • The bound atlas has a setup — Bound atlas — quick A4 with labels is still one click. Set up bound atlas… chooses the paper (A5, A4, A3, US Letter or US Tabloid) and whether the plates carry their names, then shows the page count, the sheets included, the expected file size, the lowest and highest print density and what the file will not be (sRGB, no CMYK, no bleed, no crop marks) before anything is rendered. It finishes on the named file rather than leaving you to guess whether anything happened. Exports.
  • Graphical relief — a thirteenth map look under World › View › Map look: rich land cover, carved terrain, a dark ocean and luminous coasts, drawn from the world's saved fields. The control was called Map colors; it is Map look now, because this one changes the rendering and not only the palette. View.
  • A described region costs what it will cost — planning, preview, review and Apply now read the same terrain operations, so a description that moves ground shows its real footprint and rebuild before you press Apply, and one that only names and describes a place stays immediate. Sculpt.
  • The example world builds where every world builds — progress, elapsed time and the current stage appear in the ordinary generation dock, with Cancel and Retry beside them, and a reload reconnects to the build instead of losing it. Library.
  • Sculpt gets out of the way — Hide controls folds the panel while the tool stays active, uncovering the ground and the polar caps beneath it; previews follow the projection you chose, the two-hemisphere disk included; ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z undo and redo pending edits; and a press in Select feature inspects instead of panning the map or placing a stroke.
  • Room on a small screen — hiding the chapters in the Manuscript gives their height back to the page; Scene Studio opens in your theme, text size and density from its first frame; the readability band in Insights reads as prose again instead of showing its markup.
  • From tester reports — on Windows, clicking a cell while an edit is being saved now waits briefly for the new revision instead of reporting a change that never happened.
