Delta V15, dated 7 November 2026, comes with installers for Apple Silicon Macs, Windows x64 and Linux x64 (still glibc 2.34 or newer). Your access code carries over and the licence is unchanged (still version 2.3). Worlds made with Delta V14 open as they are, and the Library offers Update to this version for them: V15’s climate carries rain deeper inland, piles it against mountains and sharpens the rain shadows behind them, wears slopes as well as valleys and joins lakes that meet at one level, so an updated world can gain or lose forest, desert and lakes, and some rivers, towns and names can move. The world as it was is kept in the Library as a copy of its own. Continent and region maps built with Delta V14 read as out of date until you generate them again, and a region world builds its region again as it opens. Settlement Studio is retired: its scenes open their towns in the city builder. The full account is in the release notes.

  • The city builder: Open city on any settlement (its dossier, the World map, a continent or region map) opens it on the finest ground the world has there, with its coast, lakes and named rivers in place, and Make › Make a town starts from a world place or blank ground. A city grows over the land it is given, from a hamlet to a metropolis of two million: a spit town fills its spit, a delta city bridges its islands, a valley town keeps to the valley floor, a lake town reaches its island by causeway. A large city has an old town, a wall and ring road for each walled age, high roads, swallowed villages, docks, works, a well-off side, slums, parks, cemeteries and suburbs, each district with its own street pattern, and every street, quarter, gate and landmark is named in the place’s own tongue. City builder.
  • Tools for building one: paint sixteen kinds of district, draw and lock main roads, bridges and causeways, set wall circuits with gates and towers, add ages of growth, place squares and fourteen kinds of landmark, shape the ground and regenerate a single district. Every change is built at once and replans only the districts it reaches, with sixty steps of undo. A million-person city plans in under a second on a recent Mac. City builder.
  • Seventeen styles, a tilted view and VTT maps: roofs of tile, slate, thatch, shingle, lead and copper by quarter, age and climate, quays, walls, fields and hill shading, in seventeen drawn styles (three of them new: Ink and wash, Atlas colour and Survey). Tilt the city into 3D, raise the ground and the buildings and save it up to 4,096 px. Export a PNG of any size, SVG, a PDF sheet or poster, GeoJSON, an illustrated sheet or a Universal VTT battle map with walls, doors and gates. Export.
  • Your city on your world’s maps: a city you build or edit shows on the World, continent and region maps as you left it: the town carries the city’s name, its mark grows with its people, and zoomed in its outline and walls are drawn. Its quarters, walls, gates and bridges appear in the town’s dossier, and the city follows its town through rebuilds and travels in the world’s backup. World map.
  • Disc worlds: Create’s new start A disc world makes one flat, round world with an Edge, 2,000 to 7,500 km across. Choose where the sun goes (over the rim for an icy hub, seasons and nights; over the hub for a warm hub and no night), where the warmest ring falls and how wide the warm band is, and whether the sea pours over the Edge or a wall of ice stands there. Its grid is rings and spokes in km, places are given by distance and bearing from the hub, Day & night sweeps round it, and every World tool works on the disc; the Climate panel can move its rings on a built disc. Create.
  • Sharpen as I zoom: zoom into the World map past the point where its cells show and the ground on screen sharpens by itself, up to eight times the world’s own grid, on Physical relief, Biomes, Annual temperature and Annual rainfall: coasts, rivers and relief come in finer, biome edges follow the valleys and ridges, and rain shows on the windward slopes. It draws from a continent or region map you have generated where one covers the view. Narrow straits and land bridges stay open on every finer map. View › Sharpen as I zoom turns it off. Map views.
  • Languages for every nation: on a built world, Nations › Language gives any nation a built-in family or a tongue of your own, and a second language spoken in a corner, a region or half of it, toward the neighbour that speaks it. Apply redraws only the names inside its borders; ground, borders and towns stay put, and names you chose are kept. Tongues gain more stress rules and an option to show the stress with an accent mark. Edit nations.
  • Islands from your drawings and a Climate panel: Add island takes Shape › From an SVG file, and every closed shape in an Inkscape, Illustrator or Affinity drawing becomes an island; it also makes rings with lagoons, polygons and drawn shapes, with a Crisp edge that keeps a circle a circle through every rebuild. The new Climate panel turns a finished world’s rain from half to double and its warmth 10 °C either way, keeping the terrain and reporting what changed. Import also reads a Photoshop .psd layer by layer. Sculpt · Import a map.
  • Rules of the world: Advanced settings has a group for how a world works: wind strength and belts, erosion, river cutting, and where dry country, rainforest, savanna, temperate rainforest and the tree line begin. Save a set of rules under a name, use it on a later world in one step, and share it as a file. Left alone, they build the same world as before. Create.
  • A wetter, stormier planet: rain reaches deep into big continents, low winds pile up against ranges they cannot climb, and air coming down off a range dries its lee, so rain shadows behind high ranges turn to steppe and desert as behind the Andes or the Sierra Nevada. The Weather year adds heatwaves, cold snaps after the polar vortex weakens, extreme gusts, sea ice that grows, melts and drifts, icebergs and plankton blooms, and every world has an impact history whose craters the Sculptor can stamp. Moons of gas giants choose where the giant hangs, see its eclipses and light, and get Io-like tidal volcanoes. Labs.
  • Grids, lines and Measure everywhere: the World map grid goes down to 1° and 2° squares, its Lines menu marks the tropics and polar circles from your world’s own tilt and keeps lines you add, and continent and region maps gain the grid, the lines and Measure distance. Sculpt marks, pins and points stay sharp and keep their size at any zoom. Map views.
  • Measured again: on the reference M5 Pro Mac a Sketch world took 20.4 seconds, Quick 46.2 seconds and Full 145.6 seconds, against 18.8, 43.1 and 130.4 seconds in Delta V14, so a Full world takes about a tenth longer and Create still quotes about 20 seconds, 45 seconds and 2 minutes. Without the compiled climate kernel they took 22.5, 69.3 and 370.0 seconds. These are source runs on the release code from 7 November 2026, not timings of the packaged installers. Build.
  • Warnings that show the place: the World overview’s climate questions and the Planet Advisor’s findings say whose ground they are about, Show on map frames the place, and a setting repair offers Preview repair first. Maps by question opens on the question that fits the work you are doing. After a rename the World map reopens almost at once, and rivers draw about a third faster. World health.
