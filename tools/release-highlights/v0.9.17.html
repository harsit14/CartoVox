Delta V16, dated 13 November 2026, is the sixteenth and last Delta release; the next is CartoVox 1.0. It comes with installers for Apple Silicon Macs, Windows x64 and Linux x64 (still glibc 2.34 or newer). Your access code carries over and the licence is unchanged (still version 2.3). Worlds made with an earlier Delta open as they are, and the Library offers Update to this version for them: V16 makes the ground differently, so the first rebuild of an older world solves the whole world once, however small the edit, and the rebuild review says why (“CartoVox was updated”); edits after it rebuild only the area they reach. A world that set its own solar constant or closest approach takes Earth’s values for them when it is rebuilt, because both settings moved to new scales under new names. Continent and region maps built with Delta V15 read as out of date until you generate them again. The full account is in the release notes. Delta V16.1 (0.9.18), the same day, fixes a Windows launch that stopped responding on some computers; Windows testers on V16 should install it. Its worlds are V16’s, so nothing needs rebuilding (V16.1 notes).

  • Your language, everywhere: the whole app reads in German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Polish, down to progress lines, map keys, the Compendium and Write, with numbers written each language’s way. On a Mac there is a full menu bar in your language, and a new optional tutorial, Your first world in ten minutes, leads into three short next steps. The command palette sets any preference, and App settings has a search field. Settings.
  • Maps sharp at every zoom: the World map stays sharp however far you zoom, on every projection and on the political, culture, soil and vegetation maps, without flickering between sharp and blurry; its grid and lines draw at your screen’s own sharpness and their labels move clear of open panels. Continent, region and disc maps open in the World map’s own controls, continents build Finer detail here where you look, and rivers run on across every join and through lakes. The World map names its oceans, seas, ranges and summits, sized by importance, and four new map looks join line-art symbols. Map views.
  • Orbits with real numbers: the Planet panel gains an Axis wobble, tilt swings and spin locks, with seven one-click orbit presets. Your planet’s real numbers build the weather they should: sun strength is entered on Earth’s scale, the closest approach is the longitude of perihelion astronomers use, and tropical storms come from sea that is warm for its own world. A world tilted further than 24.5° takes its warmth from its own tilt. Deep Time can follow a moon of a giant planet and draw its eras by rock age and fossils. Create · Labs.
  • Villages, market towns and hill towns: villages have cottages round a green, farmhouses in hedged crofts, strip fields and a churchyard with the parsonage behind it; hamlets string along their road, forest villages stand in a clearing, and a village near a holy site gets an abbey. Market towns have burgage plots, a market house and coaching inns, hill towns wind up to the church or castle, and a town is walled only where its size or history warrants it. New Island and Season options, and a city exports as a 3D model (GLB). City builder.
  • Sculpt that replaces: Overwrite lets Paint land, Paint sea and Add island replace the ground instead of building on it, and an island drawn as a polygon, by its corners or from an SVG keeps that exact shape; on a disc, islands are the shapes you draw on the disc. New Erode, River carve and Stamp tools, Mirror, Steady hand and saved brushes. Deposits and market goods can be edited by hand and survive rebuilds, and a moved island takes its rocks and roads with it. Sculpt.
  • Render Studio furniture: seven compass roses, four scale bars, a cartouche, a legend, a neatline and border and an inset locator, each in its own ink, paper and typeface; print sizes, paper textures and a blend mode on every layer. Import your own symbol packs, scatter sea serpents, whales, krakens and galleons, and see changes in the preview as you work. Render Studio.
  • Write and the Compendium: Suggest mode, comments, and an Outline that is a planning board with a Corkboard and a Timeline; setup-and-payoff threads, character arcs, ages and lifelines; a research shelf, an ideas inbox, Read aloud, front and back matter, Parts and a glossary. Tongues gain “Say it in…” with an invented script, a phrasebook and a dictionary export; cultures keep their own calendars with festivals, houses and towns bear arms, and a name forge gives twenty names at a time. Manuscript.
  • Campaigns and the tabletop: start a campaign from five templates; the world moves with the campaign clock, news travels at courier speed and prices follow the seasons. The tabletop has a combat tracker, health bars, auras, area templates and drawing; Scene Studio lays rooms and whole dungeons from a sentence, with a room key and animated battle maps. Roll the road, multi-stop and flying journeys, inn cards, wrecks and hoards, and a treasure-map handout. Campaigns · Tabletop.
  • Exports, and where they go: a game engine pack for Unity, Unreal and Godot, a print-ready vector PDF with bleed and crop marks, a CMYK atlas PDF, an Obsidian kit, GEDCOM family trees and map tiles in a zip. Settings › Data & privacy › Where downloads go asks each time or always saves to one folder, a notice says where each file went, and every finished large map offers its file. Export.
  • Measured again: on the reference M5 Pro Mac a Sketch world took 14.6 seconds, Quick 31.1 seconds and Full 95.8 seconds, against 20.4, 46.2 and 145.6 seconds in Delta V15, so Create quotes about 15 seconds, 30 seconds and 1½ minutes. Without the compiled climate kernel they took 16.7, 53.9 and 314.9 seconds. A Full world needs about 2.5 GB less memory at its busiest and opens about twice as fast. These are source runs on the release code from 13 November 2026, not timings of the packaged installers. Build.
