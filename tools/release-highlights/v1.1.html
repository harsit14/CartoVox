CartoVox 1.1 follows CartoVox 1.0.1. An installed 1.0.1 updates itself on Mac, Windows and Linux; the download section has the new installers. How to use everything below is in the guide, and the complete account is in the full notes.

  • Discover: 47 hand-picked worlds, plus regions, discs, flat worlds, cities, ships, dungeons, scenes, tabletops and style packs, each built on your computer in one click. Share your own as a .cvxshare file. Discover.
  • An Underworld beneath every world: caverns, tunnels, underground rivers and seas at three depths, with portals on the surface and a Cutaway of the rock beneath any line. Underworld.
  • Cataclysm Lab: play a supervolcano, an asteroid, a great earthquake or a rising sea over your own seas and winds, see the towns struck, and keep it in the Chronicle or export it as an animation. Cataclysm Lab.
  • Canals, dams and borders by hand: cut shipping canals, flood reservoirs and drag borders, coasts, rivers and roads in a direct vector editor. Waterworks.
  • Contagion: start a plague, heresy, invention or rumour in a town and follow its spread along the world's routes. Contagion map.
  • Explore in 3D: fly over a continent, region or built city and export the flight as video. 3D.
  • For the table: Props Studio handouts, ships, airships, keeps and dungeons deck by deck, tokens and medallions, a story canvas, a War animator, ambience and more VTT exports. Campaign & table.
  • For the book and the lore: a multi-track Chronicle, dynasties and succession, realm dashboards, tongue scripts as fonts and names spoken aloud, beta-reader DOCX and full-wrap print covers. Write.
  • Maps and styles: .cvxstyle style packs, your own compass roses and cartouches, layered Photoshop renders, paper globes, interlocking 3D-printable tiles and a World website. Export.
  • Find your way: search everything with ⌘K, switch workspaces with ⌘1–⌘6, and choose a workspace focus that puts your work first. Workspace focus.
