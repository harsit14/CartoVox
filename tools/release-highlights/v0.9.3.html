Delta V2 was shared with invited testers on 27 September 2026. Realms gained coats of arms, relations, provinces, armies and markets; campaigns went to the table with fog of war; and the map gained new projections and exports. The full account is in the release notes.

  • Maps: Cahill and Waterman butterfly projections, a Mercator that looks like a world map, a map for every crop, herd animal and wild animal, and a key drawn on exported images. Map views.
  • Realms with a history: coats of arms, relations between every pair of realms and a web of them in the Codex, provinces, armies and fleets, and goods and markets that price what each place makes. Codex.
  • Names and places: a renamed place is renamed everywhere, including your books; name a place by what it should mean; and write about a town straight from the map. Codex.
  • Campaigns at the table: templates, a player vault, a table view with a clock, sessions, tokens and quests, fog of war on the world map, and borders that change during play. Campaigns.
  • Scenes: doors and line of sight, several floors, stepping inside a settlement's buildings, and fog of war with a players' view. Scenes.
  • Writing: the Chronicle reads by year, branching histories, family trees, checks for the dead and the destroyed, and chapters dragged to reorder. Chronicle · Manuscript.
  • Exports: a reader's companion, a print-shop atlas with bleed, map features for GIS in layers, and textured terrain for 3D tools. Everything that exports.
