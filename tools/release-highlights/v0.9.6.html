Delta V5 was shared with invited testers on 2 October 2026. Your access code and the licence (still version 2.2) carry over as they are. Three things look different the moment you open it: Create is four steps, the sentence under a control waits on hover, and the World map's toolbar floats. Inland basins near the poles are now laid out in real distances on the ground, so a world made with Delta V4 rebuilds with slightly different ground near the poles, and the app says so before an edit rebuilds it. The full account is in the release notes.

  • Create walks four steps: 1 Describe, 2 Candidates, 3 Choose, 4 Build, each with the whole sheet to itself and one gold button in the bar at its foot. Describe is your brief in eight sections down the left (Story, Planet, Land, Peoples, Tongues, Tectonics, Limits, Advanced), so it fits on one screen. Find 12 strong matches fills Candidates with World A to World L, and choosing one is a click; Choose shows it large, with its seed for the first time; Build shows Sketch, Quick, Full and a publication atlas as four pictures with their real time and memory. The step bar takes you back to any step you have done. Create.
  • Advanced settings are part of Describe, its last section, instead of a screen of their own. The twenty groups run down the side with how many settings you changed in each, search still reads all 319, and while it is open Your world so far becomes a live preview of one seed, with a Preset / Yours switch and what a build with these settings would cost. The command palette, the setup guide's Expert view and a health check's repair all land there. Advanced settings.
  • Explanations are on hover: the sentence under a control waits behind the small i beside its label from your first visit. What a step costs, what is saved and what cannot be undone still stand. Settings › Appearance › Explanations › Always show brings every sentence back. App settings.
  • The World map's toolbar floats: Map appearance, Sculpt terrain, Export and Tools are one bar at the bottom of the map. Drag its grip to put it anywhere, double-click the grip to send it home, or hide it with the switch beside Focus. Zoom, 1:1 and Focus are at the bottom right, the key and coordinates keep the bottom left, and the resolution reads at the top right. The buttons at the top left are World overview and Map layers now. The toolbar.
  • Focus and Fullscreen are one button: Focus puts the panels, map key and planet away and takes the map fullscreen; Exit focus or Esc brings them back as you left them. The toolbar.
  • Volcanoes, caves and coasts, named: Volcanoes, Karst & caves and Coastal landforms mark stratovolcanoes, calderas, geyser fields, seamounts and atolls; cave systems, sinkhole fields and lava tubes; and fjords, sounds, lagoons, barrier islands and cays. Each is named in the tongue of its land and listed in the gazetteer. A new Advanced setting, Coastal erosion, wears soft capes back to shoals; at 0, the default, coasts are exactly as before. Map views.
  • Hazards, weather and life: Seismic hazard and Tsunami exposure; Snow depth, Snow season, Monsoons, Tropical cyclones and Storms & fog; UV exposure; Vegetation types, Biodiversity, Languages and Faiths; oil and gas fields in the Deposits layer; and Layer tints, the flat stepped atlas look, under Map look. Every one works on the worlds you already have, with no rebuild. Map views.
  • Run history: Write › Chronicle › Run history advances the realms over 100 to 600 years, at a Calm, Steady or Turbulent tempo, in a few seconds and without touching terrain, climate or rivers. Wars, famines, plagues, secessions, roads and towns come from the world's own pressures and say so with numbers. Scrub the year scale to watch the borders move, click a border to ask why it is there, and pin the facts every run must keep. Chronicle.
  • What this world means: World overview opens on two to four measured findings about the world in front of you, such as its most drought-exposed region, its longest mountain border or the towns on wide floodplains, each with Show on the map and a question for the cell inspector. Decision maps.
  • Roads on a world you already have: World overview › Realms & settlements › Change adds Roads and Sea lanes, and the Sculptor has a Road tool (key W) that asks for a name and a rank. Roads you draw, and the roads in a drawing or an imported map, are real roads: they carry travellers and leave in the GeoJSON, KML and vector map. Sculpt.
  • One row of actions for a place: a town's card, the cell inspector and a settlement's dossier share Inspect, Journey from here, Journey to here, Write lore and Plan this settlement. Travel adds Pick on map, and folds season, objective and party size under Route assumptions. Travel.
  • A bound atlas that reads like a book: a designed cover, plates numbered I, II, III under running heads, a legend measured from the plates with each plate's scale and projection, and a gazetteer of every place the plates letter. A plate across the date line shows both sides of it. Ask for the book before the build with Bound book on Create's Build step. Everything that exports.
  • More ways out: the vector map can add bedrock, soils, Köppen climate, currents, winds, deltas and deposits as layers of their own; Heightmap saves 16-bit or 8-bit greyscale elevation; Greyscale saves any map in plain grey; and Places and roads (KML) carries the roads and sea lanes. Every Export row is a short name beside its format badge, and no label ends in “…” any more. Everything that exports.
  • The Library opens on Continue: the world you were working in, large, with its thesis, its figures and one Resume. Start something new is a compact row below your worlds, every world card is the same size, and Trash folds away while saying what it holds. Library.
  • Writing: the Manuscript's toolbar is three labelled groups, Chapter tools, Format and Book tools, and the book selector carries a Manage menu. Continuity now reads your prose: a name one slip off, a stated distance or journey time too short for the map, a wrong realm or kind, a harvest out of season. Each finding has Accept, a line in codex/Continuity_Log.md can settle one in plain words, and the Compendium's 22 sections sit under five headings. Manuscript · Compendium.
  • Tools shown: Essentials or Everything is one setting, first in the Layout drawer and in App settings, and the welcome offers it once. Nothing Essentials holds back is gone: the Tools menu ends in More tools, the Compendium in More sections. App settings.
  • Safer: another website open in your browser can no longer drive CartoVox, files inside a shared world open sandboxed, and the usage report no longer sends free memory or the Python version, which the licence does not name. It now names the map views, styles and export formats you choose, and still never anything of yours. What is sent.
