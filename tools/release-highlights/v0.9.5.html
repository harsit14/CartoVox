Delta V4 was shared with invited testers on 30 September 2026. Your access code keeps working. The licence is now version 2.2, so the first start shows it once more with your code filled in; it adds vector maps, settlement plans, Compendium pages and lexicons to what is yours, and says which fonts a PDF export embeds. Settlements from a saved world are now planned around their water, coast and roads, so generate a saved one again to get the new plan, and a world made by an earlier Delta build says so before an edit rebuilds it. The full account is in the release notes.

  • A vector map for your drawing program: Vector map in the Export menu (then called Vector map for Illustrator, Affinity, Inkscape…) writes one SVG with named layers, or a vector PDF, in the projection and turn you are looking at. Sea depths, land, lakes, realm fills and borders, contours, coastline, rivers, roads, the graticule, settlements and places of power are each a layer of paths you can restyle, every name is text you can edit, and the paper runs from A4 to A0. Everything that exports.
  • The Export menu is sorted into Pictures, Vector and print, Games and tabletop, 3D and printing, GIS and data, and Provenance and backup, with a format badge on every row, the seven projections on one row, and a fade that says More formats below when something is out of sight. It works from the keyboard and can be read in Parchment Light. Everything that exports.
  • Take a saved world's realms away and keep its towns: the World drawer (now World overview) has a Realms & settlements row that says whose realms these are. Clear generated realms keeps every town, road and port; Change chooses the realms and the towns separately, down to a physical world with no peoples, and prices the rebuild from the world's own last build. A rebuild that changes only realms, towns, roads or the nation brief now starts at the peoples, so terrain, climate, rivers and biomes stay exactly as they were. Sculpt.
  • Draw your own realms on a world you already have: the Sculptor's Realm tool traces a border and names the realm and its capital, and Found the realms you drew rebuilds the peoples. Select a realm and choose Remove to take one away: its capital stays as an ordinary town, and its neighbours and the wilderness take its ground. Sculpt.
  • Settlements from your worlds, easy to find again: World › Scenes has New settlement from your world beside Create scene, and towns, capitals and ports on the map, and cities in the gazetteer, have Plan this settlement. It opens Settlement Studio on that place, and the scene you save is linked back to the town. Settlements.
  • Towns built around their water: the river is laid down before the streets, at the width of the reach by the town, and the streets stop at quays. Bridges cross at the narrowest point, from a street to a street. There is one wall, with gates where the roads come in. Towns by the sea or a lake have that shore, and a seaport gets a harbour. Farmland lies in belts along the roads out of town, and rivers, lakes and bays carry the names your world gave them. Saved scenes keep their old plan until you generate them again. The plan comes from the world.
  • The Codex is now the Compendium: Write › Compendium, with the Obsidian vault, Scrivener and Ellipsus bundles under the new name. Typing codex in the command palette still takes you there, and vaults exported under the old name still sync back. The Compendium.
  • The Lexicon: Write › Compendium › Lexicon shows every root in your world's tongues, what it means, whether it begins or ends names, and every name built on it. Change a meaning, respell a root after seeing every rename it causes, add roots of your own or lock the ones you love, and export the lexicon as CSV or Markdown. The Lexicon.
  • Allowed and Forbidden language families that work: one row per family, Allowed, Any or Never, with a sentence saying what the world will do. A saved world changes its families at the top of the Lexicon, and only the names are rebuilt: names in a tongue you took away are drawn again, and names you chose stay yours. Storms, manor seats and rulers now keep to the world's own tongues. Nations and tongues.
  • Forest painting shows as you paint: a Forest stroke hatches the land it reaches green, and a Clearing stroke brown. The status line counts the land painted, and a stroke that only crosses the sea says it changes nothing. The woodland itself grows when the world is re-simulated; Quick look previews it. Sculpt.
  • The reading under the map is no longer cut off: the distance scale comes first, the height and biome under your pointer get the room they need, and on a small window they take a second row. The cell inspector.
  • Worlds say which build made them: before an edit rebuilds a world made by an earlier Delta build, the world page, the Sculptor's edit plan and the rebuild review say so, because the newer generator gives different continents and coasts. Mark ground with Preserve, or build a comparison draft first. Worlds from before Delta V4 read as Made with an earlier Delta build. Sculpt.
