Delta V7 was shared with invited testers on 12 October 2026, with installers for Apple Silicon Macs, Windows x64 and Linux x64. Your access code carries over, and 500 new codes join it. The licence is unchanged (still version 2.3). Worlds made with Delta V6 open as they are, but this build lays out water, lakes and climate differently, so the same seed rebuilt here gets different ground: the Library's Update to this version rebuilds a world when you choose to, keeping the old copy beside it. The full account is in the release notes.

  • Your brief wins over the planet type: Create composes every world as land shape, then planet type, then your brief, then Advanced. Warm, Cold, Lush, Arid and the other graded choices move the world from where the shape and planet put it, and each control's middle (From the planet or From the world) keeps the world's own value. Describe says where every value came from, and Advanced opens on the combined values, with your own edits still winning. Create.
  • A chosen card keeps its promise: candidates are drawn from the same settings their world is built from, and the built world is checked against the card you chose. Every land shape checks for what makes it that shape, Best brief match puts the safest matches first, and a Prefer box beside Must have and Avoid raises a world's match without ruling any world out. Candidates.
  • Lakes settle and rivers run downhill: lakes fill, spill and merge hollow by hollow, rivers follow the actual ground, erosion follows real catchments, and moisture is carried round the globe without losing water. Inspecting a lake shows its water budget, and each lake is named Freshwater or Salt from its own regime. The cell inspector.
  • Seasons belong to the place: climate follows the finished terrain and is read at each place's own height. Southern places have southern seasons, Mediterranean means a dry summer, and monsoon winds turn with the seasons.
  • A river through a lake is one river, named above and below the lake and ranked by its whole basin. Held passes are real cols on every road over them.
  • History and trade follow the ground: Chronicle's Living History conserves food; famine, migration and expansion follow places people can reach; goods cost what their journey costs; and every port has a sea lane. Chronicle.
  • Countries, states and counties: political divisions are ready by default, and an older world fills in missing ones from its saved realm territories. States and Counties are map layers under Life & settlement, and Political hierarchy chooses country → state → county, kingdom → duchy → county or your own titles. Map views.
  • Play inside CartoVox: a native tabletop with a GM desk, tokens, grid snapping, measurement, HP and conditions, turn order, dice, fog, live token sight and doors, and Open player window for a second display at the table. Open it from a scene's Open tabletop, Play in the scene library, or Tabletop in the Library or Campaign. The player view leaves out GM notes, private rolls and unrevealed tokens. Scenes.
  • Make, a fifth workspace: beside Create, World, Write and Library, Make offers three illustrated paths to a finished piece (a playable region, maps in a chapter, or finished map artwork). Each path is three steps that open the editor you need; opening a step never creates content or exports a file by itself.
  • Find a layer, keep a view, shortlist places: Map layers searches every layer by name, description and category, with All, Favourites and Recent; star a layer to keep it. Saved views keeps named views of the World map (layer, pan and zoom, projection, appearance, filters, borders and places) that travel in project backups, and Find places filters the world's places by kind, realm, biome, elevation, climate, population and more, with up to 30 saved shortlists. Map views.
  • Closing during a build asks first: if a world is being built, rebuilt or drawn when you close the window or quit, CartoVox says how far along it is and asks; Cancel keeps it open so the build can finish. The question no longer freezes the app.
  • Smaller installers: the offline starter worlds are gone. A starter world you already saved stays an ordinary editable project in your Library, with its notes and backups. Library.
  • 500 more access codes: Delta V7 accepts 551 codes, up from 51. D-052 to D-551 are new, so Delta can open to the whole Discord server, and every earlier code still works.
  • Also new: three one-click themes in App settings (Abyssal Cartographer, Ember Forge and Sage Observatory); a new Atlas Compass logo; and Atlas VTT collections that Scene Studio and Settlement Studio export and Scene Studio imports back. App settings · Scenes.
