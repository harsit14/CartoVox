0.9.2 is Delta V1, shared with invited testers on 24 September 2026. It opens with a personal access code and reports how it is used, and most of what it adds answers what testers asked for: imported maps that keep what you drew, sculpting that changes the ground as you paint, and more ways to take a world to the table. The full account is in the release notes.

  • A Delta access code opens it — a short personal code, CVD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX, in place of the beta keys, which no longer work. The first unlock registers the computer once over the internet, and one code unlocks up to three computers. Taking part includes a usage report — sessions, the tools and exports you use, errors and how long work takes — that never contains your worlds, maps, lore, manuscripts or anything you type. First launch.
  • Imported maps keep what you drew — a GeoJSON file brings its rivers, roads, towns and realm borders as well as its coastline; traced realms come back as traced, and realm borders come out smooth. A map whose land is one flat colour now gets hills and ranges raised inside your coastline, a flat-coloured sea gets shelves, basins and trenches, and whatever you shaded keeps your shading. An Import report lists what was preserved, transformed, uncertain and not carried before you commit. Import a map.
  • A brief that takes no for an answer — Mountains can be None: flat land to sculpt; Civilisation adds Only the places I place and No peoples: physical world; how ragged the coasts are is a setting; and Advanced settings can be searched and undone, each listing the settings it pulls with or against. The World Brief.
  • Tongues in your own sounds — a tongue's card gains Sounds: its consonants and vowels (a letter may be a cluster, so str is one consonant), its syllable shapes and its stress. Every root is then spelled in them, with the same word for the same meaning wherever it appears. The World Brief.
  • Sculpting, rebuilt — brushes change the terrain live as you paint, in any projection, with pen pressure and land-only, sea-only and keep-coastline filters. Mountains, Terrace and Level land join the tools, Flatten now brings raised ground down to its target height, and Quick look shows in seconds how rain, rivers and biomes would answer your pending edits. Sculpt.
  • More ways out of the world — the Export menu adds named places for globe tools (KML), a hexcrawl sheet, an interactive map to hand to players, map tiles for GIS and printable solid terrain; a scene exports to virtual tabletops, a campaign makes a player handout, a route downloads as GPX, and render bundles carry the geography as vectors. Everything that exports.
  • On the World map — Hotspots that open a scene, a Codex page or a chapter; a distance scale re-measured as you zoom, pan and turn; Stack in the split view, drawing one map over another with opacity or Multiply; and two maps of the sea, Marine zones and Fisheries & ocean food. The map.
  • Writing and lore — Insights credits dialogue by speaker and reads the book by point of view, and saves its tables as a spreadsheet or a Word appendix; scene checks read the sky, know when characters are born and catch a name its tongue could never form. Imported lore reads Markdown front matter and brings its images, and a relationship can say when it began and ended. Manuscript · Codex.
  • Travel and settlements — the journey planner adds an ox wagon, a relay courier and a marching column; the settlement editor outlines any building an edit cut off from the streets, and Reconnect cut-off streets lays lanes back to them as one step. Travel · Settlements.
