REFINEMENTS
The display atlas is 10% larger and shifted left for balance. Its supporting map lines are simpler.
The compass's longest ray is shortened from 58 to 42 design units, and its arms are sturdier.
Survey ticks are reduced from nine to five; terrain contour paths from four to two. The route is quieter.
The wordmark uses a bespoke pointed V with a directional upper serif; r–t, o–V, and V–o spacing was refined.
The compact atlas is 20% larger. Its C band is thicker and the compass loses the dark facet that disappears at small sizes.
The standard app icon now uses the compact mark. The detailed display icon is retained separately.

VARIANTS AND USE
Primary — detailed logo for light backgrounds.
Reverse — detailed logo for dark backgrounds.
Signature — full logo with the optional “Worlds into words” tagline.
Emblem — detailed symbol without the name; use where fine map details remain legible, preferably 160 pixels or larger.
Compact Symbol — stronger symbol for small interface placements, supplied in teal and light versions.
App Icon — compact mark on a deep teal rounded plate, with PNGs at 16, 24, 30, 32, 38, 40, 48, 64, 128, 192, and 1024 pixels.
App Icon Display — the detailed emblem on a deep teal plate for large display applications.
Ink / White — single-color variants with transparent knockouts. These SVG variants use a luminance mask; matching PNGs are included.
Favicon — ICO containing 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 pixel sizes.
Preserve proportions and leave clear space around the artwork.
