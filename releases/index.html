CartoVox 1.0.1

More landforms to stamp and to find, water you can shape in the city builder, and a quieter, steadier app.

The first update to CartoVox 1.0. The Sculptor's Stamp gains seventeen landforms, from cenotes and tepuis to canyons, drumlins and salt flats, and worlds now find and name them where their rock, climate, water and ice put them. The city builder can fill water to a height, so islands no longer need tracing by hand, and the water and land you shape can be chosen again, changed, moved or deleted; towns now fill small islands, and Generate city now plans the ground you draw. On the Polyhedral and butterfly World maps, rivers, roads and borders stop at each triangle's edge instead of crossing the gaps, and names read upright. Disc, region and flat worlds show themselves taking shape while they build, with closer time and memory estimates. CartoVox opens quietly, gains a Safe graphics switch on Windows and Linux, and says on its title screen when a new version is out.
