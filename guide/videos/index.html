Describe the world, compare candidates, and build a Sketch.

Read the guide: Creating a world →

Read the transcript: Create your first world
  1. Choose Generate a new world, then Writing and world building.
  2. Describe the world in a few sentences.
  3. Review the recognised ideas. Apply all to use them in the brief.
  4. Find strong matches to compare possible starting worlds.
  5. Search time omitted. Compare the candidates and choose a favourite.
  6. Continue to review your chosen seed.
  7. Continue to Build when the seed suits your world.
  8. Choose Sketch world for a quick first pass.
  9. Start the build. The app simulates the world in the background.
  10. Build time omitted. Choose Open in studio to explore your world.

02 / World · Map layers36 sec

One world, many maps

Switch between map styles and read the same geography.

This video could not start. Try the player controls or download the MP4 below.

Read the guide: Map layers and views →

Read the transcript: One world, many maps
  1. Open Map layers to explore different readings of your world.
  2. Choose Cartography for illustrated map styles.
  3. Choose Tolkien sepia for a warm, ink-on-parchment atlas.
  4. The style changes. Your coastlines and places stay connected.
  5. Switch to Swiss analytical relief to read the shape of the land.
  6. Try Climate & seasons for maps that explain the landscape.
  7. Choose Annual rainfall to compare wet and dry regions.
  8. Use the scale to read the values behind the colours.

Read the guide: The cell inspector →

Read the transcript: Understand a place’s climate
  1. Click a place on the map to open its cell inspector.
  2. Read the elevation, temperature and annual rainfall for this cell.
  3. Find “Why is the climate here?” in the inspector.
  4. Choose Explain this cell to inspect the climate model.
  5. Compare local rainfall with the latitude’s baseline and contributing factors.
  6. Read where the air came from and what it crossed on the way.

04 / World · Travel34 sec

Plan a believable journey

Choose two places, calculate a route, and read what it demands.

This video could not start. Try the player controls or download the MP4 below.

Read the guide: Journey Laboratory →

Read the transcript: Plan a believable journey
  1. Open World → Travel to plan a route through the saved terrain.
  2. Choose a starting place and a destination on the same landmass.
  3. Keep On foot, then check the season and party size.
  4. Calculate journey to find the route and its costs.
  5. The route follows the world’s terrain, roads and crossings.
  6. Scroll down for distance, travel time and the elevation profile.
  7. Read the daily travel journal before you leave.

Read the guide: The manuscript desk →

Read the transcript: Write beside your world
  1. Open Write → Manuscript to start a book in this world.
  2. Choose New book and give it a title.
  3. Add a chapter to begin drafting.
  4. Write your opening. The chapter saves as you work.
  5. Type [[ and the start of a place name to search your world.
  6. Select the place to add a link inside your prose.
  7. Wait for Saved. Your chapter and its place link are kept together.

Take your maps with you

Save an atlas plate and export editable vector artwork.

This video could not start. Try the player controls or download the MP4 below.

Read the guide: Export formats →

Read the transcript: Take your maps with you
  1. Open Map layers → Atlas plates to browse your finished sheets.
  2. Choose Illustrated world to view the complete atlas plate.
  3. Open Download, then save the Original PNG.
  4. Open Export on the bottom dock for other file formats.
  5. Choose Vector map, quick to download editable SVG artwork.
  6. Keep the PNG as a finished image; edit the SVG in a vector editor.
