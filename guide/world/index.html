The guide · World

The map, the atlas and the road.

World is where a finished world lives. Map reads it and changes it, Atlas draws it as illustrated plates, and Travel plans the road. Campaign and Scene Studio, for play, have their own page.

World › Map

Read the map

The map is the page. At the top left, World overview summarises the world and Map layers opens every view. Saved views and Find places sit beside them. The toolbar along the bottom holds Map appearance, Sculpt terrain, Export and Tools, and you can drag it anywhere. Zoom, Fit and full screen are at the bottom right, and the key is at the bottom left.

Change the view

Where: World › Map › Map layers

Over ninety views share one geography, grouped as terrain and geology, climate, water and atmosphere, society, cartography, atlas plates and projections. Type in Search all map layers to find one. On rainfall, biome, soil, realm and similar views, the key is a filter: choose classes to show or hide. A few views answer a question for a story, such as Water security & drought resilience, the agricultural calendar and floodplain exposure.

Click a place and ask why

Where: click the map; Tools › Explain climate; Tools › World facts

Click a land cell for its height, climate, rivers, soil, bedrock, biome, realm and any model warnings. A place's card has its Climate chart, an almanac of daylight, moons and tides, and the night sky. Explain climate shows why a place is wet or dry. World facts lists the world's records and statistics. Select a town, river or realm to rename it everywhere it appears.

Change how it looks

Where: World › Map › Map appearance

Choose a map look, from shaded relief and Satellite to Vintage atlas, Ink and wash and Night atlas. Set the shallows, the season's snow, the projection and the detail level. Sharpen as I zoom keeps the map crisp close up. In 1.1, pick a Realm palette for political maps, use Turn view and a central meridian to recentre the world, and compare a familiar landmass at the map's scale with True size New in 1.1 · coming soon.

Use or make a style pack New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: Map appearance's looks; style packs in Discover

A .cvxstyle file carries a whole look: colour ramps, map furniture, symbols and fonts (where their licences allow). Preview the listed changes before you apply them, and undo them together. To make your own, choose a look, build colour ramps with stops in real units, sketch symbols on a grid, then export the pack.

Find a tool

Where: World › Map › Tools

Tools holds the instruments: Measure, Landmarks, Deposits, Climate Lab, Weather, Sky & planet, Tectonics, Erosion Lab, Coast Lab, Continents & regions, Nations, Name forge, Trade flows and more. Most have a single-key shortcut, listed with ?. Your workspace focus puts your tools first.

Closer maps

Continents, regions, 3D and the sky

Open a continent or region at finer detail

Where: Tools › Continents & regions

Build any continent, or a region you draw, as its own finer map, with every view, its own borders, roads and names, and large exports up to 16,384 pixels. Esc goes back to the world.

Fly over it in 3D New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: on a continent or region map, Tools › Explore in 3D; a built city's More menu

Fly with the keys and mouse, and set the time of day and the season. Click a pin for its card. Save viewpoints as waypoints, play the route, and export the flight as MP4 or WebM, up to 4K.

Read the sky and the weather

Where: Tools › Sky & planet; Tools › Weather

The weather year plays on the map, with storms, isobars and the aurora. Sky & planet lists eclipses, prints star charts and lets you draw your own constellations. In 1.1 it adds an orrery and a spinning-globe export, rings included New in 1.1 · coming soon.

Below ground New in 1.1 · coming soon

The Underworld

Every world has an Underworld, read from its own rock, water and heat: caverns, tunnels, underground rivers and seas at three depths. Find it under Map layers › Terrain & geology › Underworld. Turn on Underworld portals in landmarks to see the cenotes, caves and shafts that lead down. Underground features can be found in search alongside places on the surface. To see the rock beneath a line, draw it with Measure and choose Cutaway, then save the block as SVG, PNG or PDF.

Sculpt terrain

Change the world

Sculpt terrain, then review

Where: World › Map › Sculpt terrain

Raise, lower, paint, stamp landforms, move an island, carve a river or protect an area. Edits stay pending until Review & re-simulate, which shows the affected area and systems before you apply them. Small edits rebuild locally. New islands, opened straits and ice-worn coasts rebuild the whole world. Build comparison draft builds the result beside the original. Quick look previews a draft.

Cut a canal or dam a valley New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: Sculpt terrain › Road › Kind › Waterworks › Canal or Dam

Click points across the land and press Enter. A canal between two waters opens a shipping route. A dam floods a reservoir behind it, and the review lists the places and roads that change. After you apply it, select the waterwork on the map to read its route, reservoir and seasonal flow downstream.

Move a border, coast or road by hand New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: Tools › Edit vectors

Drag the points of a realm or province border, a coast, a river, a road or a sea lane. Lines can snap to ridges and rivers, and the area and population update as you go. Roads and borders change at once. River and coast edits tell you when the terrain needs a rebuild. In 1.0.1, nations and rivers are edited from Tools › Nations and Sculpt.

Play a disaster in the Cataclysm Lab New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: World › Map › Tools › Cataclysm Lab

Choose a supervolcano, an asteroid, a great earthquake or a rising sea, choose a spot on the world, continent or region map, and play it. Tsunamis cross your own seas, ash drifts on your own winds, and the panel lists the towns struck. Nothing changes until you keep it. A dated event goes to the Chronicle, a crater or caldera goes to Sculpt for review, and the event exports as a GIF, WebP, MP4 or WebM animation.

Test a different climate

Where: Tools › Climate Lab

Rebuild the world's climate under changed rules, such as a warmer planet or wetter oceans, and compare the result before you keep it.

Artwork

Render Studio

Render Studio turns the open world into finished map art. An art direction sets the whole look in one click, and you can then change the palette, linework, lettering and atmosphere. Turn the terrain into editable drawing layers, stamp symbols, letter realms and rivers, and add a compass rose, scale bar, cartouche, legend and border. Nothing you draw changes the world's data. In 1.1 New in 1.1 · coming soon: design your own compass roses, borders and cartouches, import Wonderdraft symbol folders, and download a layered Photoshop file up to 8K.

World › Atlas

The illustrated atlas

Atlas holds the world's illustrated plates (the world, each continent and each region), labelled or blank, in looks from CartoVox and Parchment to Woodcut, Watercolour, Medieval portolan, Admiralty chart and Night atlas. If you skipped plates at build time, Render remaining plates draws them without re-simulating. Edit plate moves, hides or swaps any stamp and sets the frame and title. Edit labels moves the names.

World › Map › Export

Everything that exports

Export on the map's toolbar holds every way out. Your focus decides which come first.

  • Images and print: the current view as PNG, large maps up to 16K, vector maps as SVG with named layers or vector PDF, and a bound atlas PDF with a cover, contents, legend and gazetteer.
  • Books and sites: a World Bible as PDF or EPUB, an offline web map, and in 1.1 a World website with linked dossiers, timeline and relationship diagrams New in 1.1 · coming soon.
  • Data and tools: GeoJSON, GeoPackage, GeoTIFF, STL and OBJ terrain, an Obsidian vault, a game engine pack for Unity, Unreal or Godot, and a .cartovox project backup.
  • For paper and hands New in 1.1 · coming soon: paper globe gores, interlocking square or hex terrain tiles as STL and 3MF with an assembly sheet, and schematic route maps.
  • For the table: a treasure map handout, hexcrawl sheets, and in 1.1 in-world props New in 1.1 · coming soon.

World › Travel

Journeys, and things that spread

Plan a journey

Where: World › Travel

Choose From and To, a way of travel and a season, then Calculate journey. Routes weigh terrain, roads, rivers, currents, borders and hazards, and report the distance, days, supplies, dry stretches and tolls. Stops on the way runs one journey through several towns. Save a route to use in a campaign.

Watch a plague, rumour or idea spread New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: World › Travel › Journey setup › Contagion map, or Tools › Contagion map

Choose what is spreading (a plague, a heresy, an invention or a rumour) and the town it starts in, then Let it spread. Follow its arrival along local routes on the world, a continent or a region, and read the dates and the towns reached. Keep the outbreak as an era in the Chronicle, which you can undo.
