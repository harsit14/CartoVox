The guide · Write

Write in the world you built.

Write has three sections: Chronicle for history, Compendium for lore, and Manuscript for the book. All three know the map, so a place, a date or a name links back to the world it came from.

Write › Chronicle

Chronicle: the world's history

Import the generated timeline, then add eras, settlements, wars, borders, migrations, routes and disasters as points, lines or areas on the map, and play them over the world. Later eras keep the lasting consequences. Give a character a birth year, and their scenes and events show their age. Lifelines draws one bar per character. Events kept from the Cataclysm Lab and the Contagion map arrive here too New in 1.1 · coming soon.

Compare lanes of history New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: the Chronicle's multi-track timeline

Work across five lanes. Connect causes to their effects, compare a dated chapter with the realms, wars and lives around it, and read warnings about impossible travel or founding dates. Convert a date between cultures to see its festivals, seasons and moon phases.

Write › Compendium

Compendium: lore you can link

The Compendium holds a page for every named thing (realms, towns, rivers, peoples, species, faiths) with measured facts beside your own words. The generator leaves the prose blank for you rather than inventing it. Describe everything lists the fields still open, and Rename everything renames across the whole world on one sheet. Your lore adds your own pages and can start from a template, such as a writer's bible or a game master's world book.

Shape languages and names

Where: Write › Compendium › a tongue's page; Lexicon; Name forge

Every people speaks a tongue with its own sounds, roots and script, and names come from them. The Lexicon steers a tongue's words, and Name forge finds names by meaning. In 1.1 the Tongue studio edits signs and grammar, exports the script as a font and traces sound changes from parent to daughter tongues. A small speaker beside a name says it aloud, using a voice made on your computer New in 1.1 · coming soon.

Read a realm's people, trade and dialects New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: a realm's page › its dashboard

See urban and rural population, ages and classes, harvests, military upkeep and trade, plus a dialect map of how words change across the realm. Realm and town pages describe cuisine and material culture from local produce and trade.

Chart dynasties, faiths and factions New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: Write › Compendium › Your lore

Chart families, marriages and reigns, choose a succession law and see who is heir and why, then print a family poster. Design a magic system, a pantheon or a faction's organisation. Each becomes a searchable page with linked diagrams that can go into the World Bible.

Make your own species

Where: the Species editor (from search, the Compendium or a biome)

Create species, describe their habitats and relationships, and see where they can live. In 1.1, explore a biome's food web and estimate what follows if a species arrives or disappears New in 1.1 · coming soon.

Bind the lore as a book

Where: Write › Compendium › World Bible

Download a World Bible as PDF or EPUB with atlas maps, place pages, history, peoples and tongues. The player edition keeps to what the campaign has revealed.

Write › Manuscript

Manuscript: write the book

A word processor with the world beside it. Choose New book, then Add chapter, and write. It saves as you go. The toolbar groups Chapter tools, formatting and Book tools.

Link places and pin scenes

Where: type [[ in a chapter; the World panel

Type [[ and a name to link a place, person or page. ⌘-click a link to open its page beside the chapter. Pin a scene to a place and a day in the world's year, and the panel shows that day's date, season, moon, weather, realm and travel time from the last scene.

Check continuity and style

Where: Run checks; Insights

Run checks flags facts out of time, names spelled two ways, impossible travel and festival days. It finds supported problems, but it can't promise the whole book agrees with the world. Insights measures sentence shape, dialogue, adverbs and repetition. In 1.1, after you rename a place, review every chapter's mentions in context and undo them together New in 1.1 · coming soon.

Plan the book

Where: the Plan and Draft tabs; Book › Outline

Set a word target and a finish date, and give chapters a status and colour labels. The Outline is a planning board with story-structure templates, a story-time timeline and a corkboard. The Draft tab keeps an ideas inbox and a research shelf.

Import, export and print

Where: Book tools; Style; Read

Import Word, PDF or Markdown drafts. Export Word with real footnotes, EPUB, Kindle, HTML, Markdown or Scrivener. Style sets drop caps, front and back matter, trim and running heads. Read previews printed pages.
