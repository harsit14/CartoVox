On disk

What a world writes to disk

Each world is a folder in the CartoVox data folder, in an open layout. No account is involved. To move the folder, use Settings › Data & privacy › Change save folder. The CARTOVOX_DATA_DIR environment variable overrides it.

File or folderWhat it holds
world_data.npzEvery simulated field as a NumPy array: elevation, climate, water, soils, biomes and more
world_summary.json · WORLDBOOK.mdThe measurements as data, and the measured dossier
world_config.jsonThe full configuration, with the recipe you chose and the simulation version
name_registry.json · overrides.jsonEvery name and its identity · your terrain edits
plates/ · city_plans/Illustrated sheets and city plans
codex/The Compendium as an Obsidian vault, with the books in novels/, one Markdown file per chapter
simulation_health.jsonScored checks of the simulation, with locations
revisions/Restore points made by applied edits
exports/What you exported

Display caches are rebuildable, left out of backups and trimmed to a per-world budget. Deleted worlds wait in worlds/.trash/.

Questions

Common questions

A button is greyed out

Hover it, or reach it with the keyboard, and it says why. Usually no brief has been written yet, or an attached map is still waiting for its coastline check.

How long will a build take?

Create's Build step gives the time, memory and disk for your exact choices, and learns from your own builds. On a fast Mac a Sketch world takes about 15 seconds, Quick about 30 and Full about 80, without illustrated plates. You can keep working while a build runs.

Why is the map a rectangle?

The world is simulated on a sphere and drawn flat: it wraps east to west, and the poles are real poles. A long, thin landmass near a pole is usually the projection. Check it on the globe or with Measure. Try another projection in Map appearance, or use Tools › Turn view to bring a continent off the edge.

I changed the terrain and nothing else changed

Edits wait until Review & re-simulate and then Apply and re-simulate. Build a comparison draft to see exactly what will move before you commit.

I rebuilt at finer detail and the coast changed

That's expected. The broad geography stays, but coasts, rivers, towns and names can move on a finer grid. The rebuild is a separate project, so the world you chose stays as it was.

Where did my world, or my panel, go?

Deleted worlds stay in Library › Trash for 30 days. A missing tool is often hidden by Tools shown › Essentials in Settings › Workspace & layout, and Help's tours point to the rest.

Does CartoVox send anything?

Nothing about you. It checks for new versions, and you can turn that off. Worlds and writing stay on your computer. A tabletop invitation, when you start one, serves the player view on your local network until you revoke it. See Privacy.

Can I sell what I make?

Yes. You may publish and sell the worlds, maps and writing you make. You may not redistribute the app itself. See the licence.

How do I report a bug?

Use Settings › Updates & support › Report a bug on Discord. Include the seed, the detail level and any Advanced settings you changed. Copy app info adds the version and platform only.

Read before you lean on a number

Known limits

CartoVox is a worldbuilding model, not a forecast. The app documents its approximations in detail. The short version:

  • Desktop first. Phone layouts are for reviewing a world, not building one.
  • Drawing density is not simulation detail. Sharper zoom and Render Studio draw the same saved cells. Only a finer rebuild adds detail, as a separate project.
  • Seasons, not days. Climate uses seasonal samples, currents are wind-driven only, and rivers come from a regional model rather than fluid dynamics. Tectonics is a kinematic model. Deep Time moves crust for hundreds of millions of years without solving mantle convection.
  • Lore is a proposal. Generated names and lore are starting points, and resources are prospects rather than proven reserves. Continuity checks catch supported problems, not every disagreement between prose and world.
  • Rebuilds can move things. Local rebuilds keep the rest of the world, but can't predict every redirected river. A newer version or a finer grid can change coasts and towns. Names and your work follow the places that survive.
  • Reproducible physics. The same seed, settings and simulation version give the same world. Pixel-identical rendering across computers is not promised.
  • Local play. Tabletop invitations work on your local network, not over the internet unless you add a VPN.
