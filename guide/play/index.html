The guide · Campaign & table

Take the world to the table.

Campaign keeps your game's secrets and what the players know. The tabletop runs the session, and Scene Studio and the city builder make the battle maps. They all use the same world as the map.

World › Campaign

Start a campaign

A world with no campaign offers five starting points built from it: a sandbox hexcrawl, intrigue in a capital, a quest across a realm, a sea voyage and a one-shot. Choose one and Start this campaign, or use New campaign for a blank one. A campaign keeps its own player map, where unknown places stay faded until you reveal them, along with notes, sessions and roll tables. Present player view shows the players' map with none of your controls.

Prepare a session

Where: the campaign's session board

Each session has Prep (a strong start, secrets and clues) and a printable session sheet. Board cards carry progress clocks, and the Today card gives the weather where the party stands. Every settlement has services and townsfolk, and every market has coins and prices. In 1.1 you can stamp points of interest and terrain on a hexcrawl sheet, and they survive a rebuild New in 1.1 · coming soon.

Tabletop

Play at the tabletop

Open tabletop puts a scene on screen with tokens, fog, measuring areas and dice. Dice can keep the highest or lowest, as in 2d20kh1. The turn order is a combat tracker with hit points, conditions and group initiative. Quick NPC names someone the party might meet. In 1.1, Ambience plays local sound to suit the scene, the time and the weather New in 1.1 · coming soon.

Let players join from their own devices

Where: the tabletop's Invite players

Players on your local network open the invitation on a phone, tablet or computer. They can move their own token, pin notes, roll dice and propose a journey, as far as you allow. Nothing moves under the fog, and your moves at the desk win. To play over the internet, use a VPN such as Tailscale. Revoke the invitation to end it.

Resolve a clash, or animate a war

Where: the campaign's Battle simulator; War animator New in 1.1 · coming soon

The battle simulator settles a clash from the forces and the ground, and the report opens at the outcome. In 1.1 the War animator tells a whole war on the map. Set the dates and sides, then the marches, sieges and blockades, and play them along the world's own roads and sea lanes. Export it as a dated video or GIF with losses and a legend.

World › Scene Studio

Scenes, ships and dungeons

Scene Studio draws a room, a settlement or an encounter map, linked to a place in the world or standing alone. From the linked place lays out a battle map in any season. The Room key numbers every room with a line for the game master.

Build a ship, keep or dungeon New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: World › Scene Studio › Generate a ship or dungeon

Choose what to build (a galleon, an airship, a keep or a dungeon, among others) and a place, preview its decks or floors, and create the scenes. Stairs, doors, room keys, encounters and lights carry into play, and ship hulls have curved walls on every deck. Moor a ship to a harbour scene, or print a one-page dungeon with a separate player copy.

Send a scene to your virtual tabletop

Where: the scene's Export

Export walls, doors, lights and named areas to Foundry or Universal VTT. In 1.1 you can also export to Fantasy Grounds Unity, Owlbear Rodeo and Roll20, with room numbers and optional notes New in 1.1 · coming soon.

City builder

Cities on the world's own ground

Any town opens as a city on its real coast, rivers and hills, from a hamlet to a metropolis of two million, with walls, gates, docks, quarters and names in the world's own language. Open one from a town on the map or from the Library's City builder. Paint districts, lock roads, add landmarks, tilt the city into 3D and export it as a poster or a VTT battle map. On blank ground, Fill water floods everything below a height you choose, and Generate city plans the ground you drew.

Go below the streets New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: the city builder's Underground, Gazetteer and More › Explore in 3D

Underground shows the sewers, cisterns, catacombs and tunnels beneath the streets. The Gazetteer numbers every building along its street and names the inns, the shops and their keepers. Search it by street, trade or keeper, and save it as Markdown or CSV. You can split or join plots, change single buildings and lock the landmarks you want to keep.

Props, tokens and the story canvas

Print in-world props New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: World › Campaign › Player exports › Props Studio; Export › In-world props; More on a town's or character's page

Print a town's broadsheet with its news, weather and prices, a sealed charter, a treaty, a wanted poster or a naturalist's plate, on A4 or Letter. Props Studio also makes a GM screen and fold-up paper standees, and treasure maps keep their local names. Props Studio needs a campaign first.

Plan on a story canvas New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: World › Campaign › Story canvas; also in Write › Manuscript

An endless board for a mystery or a plot. Pin sticky notes, cards for your characters, places and factions, map crops and pictures. Join them with labelled arrows and group them into frames. Hide cards from the players and check the player view before sharing. Export the board as PNG, SVG, PDF or an Obsidian canvas, or as JSON to reopen later.

Make tokens and medallions New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: Write › Compendium › Token & medallion composer

Make a transparent VTT token or a printable medallion from a portrait, a coat of arms, a creature silhouette or a monogram in a world script, with rings, banners and facing marks. Saved tokens appear on the tabletop and in props. Export a PNG, or print a sheet as a PDF.
