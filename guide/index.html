1Install

Install and first launch

CartoVox is free and works offline, with no account and no sign-in. Your worlds stay on your computer.

  1. Download the installer for your computer from the download section: an Apple Silicon Mac (macOS 14 or later), Windows 10 or 11 (x64), or Linux x64 (glibc 2.34 or newer, as a .deb or a portable folder). Each release publishes checksums, so you can check a download before you run it.
  2. Open it past the warning. The installers are not code-signed yet. On a Mac, Control-click CartoVox and choose Open, or use System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. On Windows, choose More info › Run anyway.
  3. Sign the licence. The first window is the licence. Read it, type your name beside the date and tick the box. The signature stays on your computer, and Settings › Licence shows it again.
  4. Meet the title screen. Its menu leads to Example world, New world, Library, Write, Make, Settings and Help & guides, plus Discover in 1.1 New in 1.1 · coming soon. The first time you open the app, a welcome card sits beside the menu.

CartoVox checks for a new version when it opens, at most once a day, and sends nothing about you. Turn that off in Settings › Updates & support. On a Mac and Windows it updates itself. On Linux it downloads the new package for you, and 1.1 can also update and restart in place New in 1.1 · coming soon.

2Orientation

How the app is laid out

One window holds everything. The rail on the left switches between six workspaces. The top bar shows the sections of the workspace you are in, plus search. Whatever you open, it works on the same world.

  • LibraryYour saved worlds, Continue, the trash, and in 1.1 the Series hub.
  • Discover New in 1.1 · coming soon47 hand-picked worlds, plus cities, ships and styles, each built in one click.
  • CreateBrief and Deep Time: describe a world, pick a candidate, build it. Or bring your own map.
  • WorldMap, Atlas, Travel, Campaign and Scene Studio for the open world.
  • WriteChronicle, Compendium and Manuscript: history, lore and the book.
  • MakeShort guides to a finished map, handout, site or book.

At the bottom of the rail are Review (names, continuity and open questions for this world), Settings and Help, which has the illustrated manual and guided tours. The CartoVox logo at the top goes back to the title screen.

Search everything with ⌘K

Where: the top bar's Ask or do anything, or ⌘K / Ctrl K anywhere

Type a place, a person, a tool or an action, such as “Underworld”, “export SVG” or a town's name, then press Enter to go there. In 1.1 the search also reaches city streets and shops, characters, Chronicle events and every line of your manuscript, and still finds a name you misspell New in 1.1 · coming soon. ⌘1–⌘6 switch workspaces New in 1.1 · coming soon (⌘1–⌘5 in 1.0.1), and ⌘, opens Settings.

Put your kind of work first New in 1.1 · coming soon

Where: the focus switch in the top bar (it reads Everything), or Settings › Workspace & layout › Workspace focus

Choose Novelist, Game master, Cartographer or Worldbuilder, or combine two. That work's tools then lead the map's Tools menu, its exports lead Export, and its sections lead each workspace. Nothing is hidden. Everything, the default, keeps the usual order. To hide specialist tools, set Tools shown to Essentials in the same section, which 1.0.1 has too.

How to read a path

Bold words are labels exactly as the app writes them. World › Map › Tools › Cataclysm Lab means the World workspace in the rail, its Map section in the top bar, the Tools menu on the toolbar at the bottom of the map, then Cataclysm Lab. If you get lost, press ⌘K and type what you want.

3Your first hour

Your first hour, step by step

Seven short tasks on one world. Do them in order or skip around. Build times depend on your computer: a Sketch world takes about 15 seconds on a fast Mac, a Full world about a minute and a half.

1. Open a finished world

Where: title screen › Example world, or the welcome card's Build the example world

The example is a real world in your own Library, ready to explore, change or delete. In 1.1 it is built from its recipe the first time you ask, which takes a minute or two New in 1.1 · coming soon. Or go to Discover and choose Build this world on any of its 47 worlds New in 1.1 · coming soon.

You should see: the world's map in World › Map, with its name in the top bar.

2. Click a place and ask why

Where: World › Map › click land, or Tools › Explain climate

Click any land cell. The inspector gives its height, climate, rivers, soil, biome and realm. Explain climate shows how much the height, the land upwind and the moisture supply each count. Every map in CartoVox comes from one simulated planet, so each answer has a cause.

You should see: a dry place explained by mountains upwind or distance from the sea.

3. Look at the same world another way

Where: World › Map › Map layers

Open Map layers and pick another view: biomes, rainfall, realms, trade or one of the illustrated looks. Type in Search all map layers to find one by name. In 1.1, try Underworld under Terrain & geology for the caverns and underground rivers beneath the world New in 1.1 · coming soon.

4. Make a world of your own

Where: Create › Brief

Under How do you want to begin?, choose Generate a world. Write a sentence or two under Your world in a few sentences, or start from a genre such as Frozen north. Choose Search candidates, then Find 12 strong matches. Click the candidate you like and continue to Build. Choose Sketch world, check the time and disk estimates, then Create Sketch world.

You should see: a new world in your Library, ready to edit. Rebuild it at Quick or Full detail when you like it.

5. Change something and see what follows

Where: World › Map › Sculpt terrain

Raise a range or carve a valley with Sculpt. In 1.1, draw a canal or a dam under Road › Kind › Waterworks New in 1.1 · coming soon. Edits stay pending until Review & re-simulate, which shows what will change before you apply it. Each applied edit leaves a revision you can restore. For something faster, play a disaster in Tools › Cataclysm Lab New in 1.1 · coming soon: nothing changes until you choose to keep it.

6. Write a chapter beside the map

Where: Write › Manuscript

Choose New book, then Add chapter. Type [[ and a place's name to link it, then pin a scene to a place and a day. The panel then shows that day's weather, season and travel time. Run checks flags names spelled two ways and travel that couldn't happen.

7. Take it to the table

Where: World › Campaign

Choose a starting point, such as a sandbox hexcrawl, and create the campaign. Open tabletop puts a scene on screen with tokens, fog and dice. Invite players lets people on your network join from a phone, tablet or computer. In 1.1, also try the Story canvas and Props Studio New in 1.1 · coming soon.

Edits you can review and undo

Re-simulating rebuilds what an edit affects. Names and your writing follow the places that survive, and places that disappear are listed for you to review. A rebuild at finer detail, or with a newer version, can move coasts and towns. Keep a comparison draft or a backup when you want the original too.
