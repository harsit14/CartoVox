One world, every use

Map it, write it, play it, build on it.

Four workspaces open the same saved world, so a name changed in one is changed in all.

Map

Map it

  • One world, many maps. Relief, climate, realms, trade, faiths, tongues, hazards and the Underworld, all from the same simulation.
  • Print-ready atlas plates. Edit any stamp, frame and title, choose a style pack, and print posters or a World Bible.
  • Art and 3D. Render Studio art directions, layered PSD up to 8K, and flights over your terrain exported up to 4K.

Write

Write in it

  • A Compendium that starts full. Realms, settlements, faiths, tongues, species and the questions they raise, written from the world itself.
  • A Chronicle of its history. Eras, wars and foundings on a multi-track timeline, played back on the map.
  • Your book, beside it. Chapters, scenes, insights, beta-reader DOCX and print covers, with every place name linked.

Play

Play in it

  • Story canvas. Pin clues, dossiers, factions and live map crops, join them with labelled string, and show players a safe view.
  • Tabletop and props. Run scenes with fog and dice, then print broadsheets, wanted posters, charters and VTT tokens.
  • Campaigns that remember. Travel with weather and prices, hexcrawls, battle reports and a war animator for your history.

Build

Build on it

  • Scene Studio. Ships, airships, keeps and dungeons deck by deck or floor by floor, in plan or isometric.
  • City builder. Villages to cities of two million, above and below ground, with a searchable street gazetteer.
  • Ready for any table. Walls, doors and lights carry into play and export to popular virtual tabletops.
