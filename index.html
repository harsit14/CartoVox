    Create. World. Write.

    • Map — inspect terrain, climate and resources.

    Quick search: about 20 seconds on a fast machine.
    Full build, all plates: about 8 min on high-end Apple Silicon; 13 min on high-end Windows.

    Disk space

    Quick: 45–100 MiB.
    Full: 180–360 MiB without illustrated plates; 337–883 MiB with all plates.

    FAQ

    Common questions.

    Does CartoVox use generative AI?

    No. Simulation and a language-aware naming system create the world. The same seed, settings and simulation version reproduce its physical geography; rendered pixels can vary.

    Does it need an internet connection or an account?

    No account. First activation needs internet; usage reports send when connected. Your worlds and writing stay local. Privacy details →

    Can I start from a map I already have?

    Yes. Import an image or heightfield, draw your coastlines, or trace a reference. CartoVox simulates the world around them.

    Can I sell what I make with it?

    Yes. Your outputs are yours, with no royalties or required credit. The app itself may not be redistributed. Licence →

    Is the simulation scientifically accurate?

    Physically motivated, deliberately simplified. CartoVox is a worldbuilding model, not a forecasting tool. Known limits →

    How big is a world on disk, and how long does it take?

    Depends on your settings and computer. See measured time and storage ranges; the app estimates each build before starting.

    Is Delta free? When will 1.0 release?

    Delta is free for invited testers. The 1.0 date and future pricing are unannounced. Request access →

    What happens to my Delta access after 1.0?

    Post-Delta access terms are unannounced. Your saved worlds and exports remain on your computer.

    What makes CartoVox different?

    Simulation, maps and writing share one world. Inspect why a place exists, then use it in your atlas, campaign and manuscript.

    Open Help in the app for the offline manual, or use the website guide. Release notes →
